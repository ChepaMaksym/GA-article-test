## Paper-level adaptation semantics

The frozen publication configuration is the individual-memory A-NTGA variant with `DF=0.999`:

1. read the main parent's mutation-operator credit vector;
2. multiply every credit by `DF`;
3. roulette-select a mutation operator proportionally to the credits;
4. mutate, repair if required, and evaluate the offspring;
5. add one credit when the offspring dominates at least one parent;
6. store the updated vector in both the main parent and offspring.

Every operator starts with credit 1. With `C_next=0.999*C+reward` and `reward` in `{0,1}`, zero is the asymptotic lower bound and `1/(1-0.999)=1000` is an invariant upper bound; no explicit clipping is required. This is a genuine within-run change of `operator_selection_probability`, not surrogate/fitness/forward-model adaptation.

## Source-native blockers

- No repository or data `LICENSE` file was found.
- The supplied A-NTGA configuration selects `UniformMultiOperator`, not the paper's credit-proportional roulette.
- In the frozen factory, the `CreditRouletteMultiOperator` and `InvCreditRouletteMultiOperator` names instantiate `CBalancedCreditRouletteMultiOperator`, not their named selector implementations. The balanced selector also uses call-count weighting and is not the paper's credit-proportional roulette.
- The source initializes stored credits at zero and adds a persistent pseudocount inside selection; the paper initializes credits at one and decays the entire vector. These transitions are not trajectory-equivalent.
- No exact paper batch configuration, 30-run seed ledger, raw run outputs, compiler/runtime lock, or immutable result archive was found.
- A local native build was not adjudicated because CMake is unavailable in the audit environment; even a successful compile would not resolve the configuration/result-provenance blockers above.

Therefore `g10=unresolved`: a clean-room MATLAB port is feasible from the paper, but the claimed source artifact cannot yet replay the published protocol without invention.
